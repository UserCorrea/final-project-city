Welcome to City Sights

An exhibition featuring a collection of different shots from the urban and suburban places of the city of Milwaukee. It explores Milwaukee city life and small moments, from the street signs to stickers on lamp posts, artist murals, the setting sun, and night.

This collection will be shown at the 207 East Buffalo St.#218, Milwaukee, WI 53202, United States a gallery in the third ward and will be available online on the collections tab. The top navigation bar on the collection site acts as a filter which will highlight the images under that category. Please enjoy the sights.

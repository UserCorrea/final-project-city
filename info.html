Introduction

In the collection, the photographs show places I pass by during the day, showing a snapshot of my life walking around, such as the highway, train track, and houses. I took them at different times and locations to showcase different atmospheres produced by the time of day, with images being taken at the same places during the day and at night, or having multiple images of the same area.

About City Sights

This exhibition focuses on the city spaces, artwork, and other areas in the city that I photographed over the past few years. The pictures were of places and objects I found interesting. I made this project to showcase these daily sights and the places we pass by every day. While for the original project I had used the Smithsonian Museum, for this one, if I were to exhibit the images in person, it would be in a gallery in Milwaukee, and the audience would be another residence. This website acts similarly to a gallery, as it also displays the images collected. For the original project I was inspired by artists such as Pascal Monaco, a digital creator, David Monodou, a graphic artist, and Luke Hickman, a printmaker and illustrator. These were my original inspirations for a project where I had to create a logo, poster, brochure, and some online posts. While this webpage took inspiration from the poster when it came to color scheme, most of the elements are different. While not relevant, I will include the poster just to show the roots of the project.
